# External High-Beta Report Reverse Audit v1

- Date: 2026-05-24
- Purpose: Absorb useful structure from short-term/high-beta reports without importing their optimism, target prices, or position sizing.
- Boundary: These reports enter `Evidence` and `Review Gates`, not direct `Action`.

## Core Rule

Use external high-beta reports to discover what to verify. Do not use them to decide what to buy.

For each report, split:

- facts from official sources
- analyst/model assumptions
- narrative multiple expansion
- price/crowding state
- monitorable events
- assumptions required for target price
- invalid hard-support claims

## Reusable Checklist

For every future short-term/high-beta report:

1. What is the target price?
2. Which assumptions are required for that target?
3. Which assumptions are official facts versus model claims?
4. Is the report relying on EPS growth, multiple expansion, or both?
5. What can be monitored within the next 1-3 quarters?
6. What would falsify the thesis?
7. Is the current price already pricing the base/upside case?
8. Is this high-trust compounder, high-odds controversial asset, right-tail optionality, or evidence-only?
